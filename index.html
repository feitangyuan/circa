中文版

Questions, bugs or ideas: email rosseneric89@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something went wrong.

Common questions

How do I pick an era?

Turn the era dial to a year from 2013 to 2019, then press the shutter. Each year follows the look of the iPhone camera from that time.

Where are my photos saved?

In your photo library, like photos from the built-in Camera app. Circa's own gallery shows only the photos you took with Circa.

The gallery is empty or asks for photo access.

Go to Settings > Circa > Photos and allow access. Circa needs it to show your Circa photos in its gallery.

How do I stop photos from recording a location?

Go to Settings > Circa > Location and choose Never. Circa keeps working, and new photos are saved without a place.

Does Circa need an account or an internet connection?

No. Circa works fully offline and does not collect any data. See the privacy policy.

What do I need to run Circa?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

If I delete Circa, are my photos deleted?

No. Your photos stay in your photo library until you delete them there.

常见问题

怎么选年代？

转动年代拨盘，选 2013 到 2019 年中的一年，然后按快门。每一年都按那一年 iPhone 相机的观感来出片。

照片存在哪？

和系统相机一样，存在系统相册里。Circa 自己的图库只显示用 Circa 拍的照片。

图库是空的，或者提示要访问照片。

打开「设置 > Circa > 照片」，允许访问。Circa 需要这个权限才能在图库里显示你用 Circa 拍的照片。

不想让照片记录地点怎么办？

打开「设置 > Circa > 位置」，选「永不」。Circa 照常能拍，之后的照片不带地点。

需要账号或联网吗？

不需要。Circa 完全离线工作，不收集任何数据，详见隐私政策。

需要什么设备？

iOS 26 或更新版本的 iPhone。

删除 Circa 会删掉照片吗？

不会。照片会一直留在系统相册里，要在相册里删除。

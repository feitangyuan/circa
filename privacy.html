Circa Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

中文版

Circa does not collect, upload or share any of your data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no network connection. Your photos and their locations stay on your iPhone.

What Circa accesses and why

  • Camera: to take photos. Camera frames are processed on your iPhone and never leave it.
  • Photo library (add): to save the photos you take to your photo library.
  • Photo library (read): to show the photos you took with Circa in its own gallery. Circa keeps a list of the identifiers of those photos so it can find them again. It does not read, copy or send any other photos.
  • Location (while using the app, optional): to write where each photo was taken into that photo, the same way the built-in Camera app does. Circa does not keep a location history and does not use your location for anything else.

Each permission is asked for by iOS, and you can turn any of them off at any time in Settings > Circa. If you turn location off, Circa keeps working and your photos are simply saved without a place.

What is stored, and where

Everything stays on your iPhone: the photos in your photo library, the list of photo identifiers, and your settings in Circa (such as the era and frame you last chose). Circa does not send any of it to the developer or anyone else. Photos may be included in your own iCloud Photos or device backups if you have turned those on; that is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing photos

Circa has no sharing feature of its own. If you share a photo from the Photos app, its location goes with it unless you turn it off in the share sheet's Options.

Deleting your data

Deleting Circa removes its photo list and settings. Photos you took stay in your photo library until you delete them there.

Tracking and third parties

Circa does not track you, does not contain third-party code that collects data, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Children

Circa does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the developer contact on Circa's App Store page, or, for beta testers, with Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

Circa 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

English

Circa 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何数据。没有账号、没有统计、没有广告，也不联网。照片和拍摄地点都只留在你的 iPhone 上。

Circa 用到什么、为什么用

  • 相机：用来拍照。取景和成片都在你的 iPhone 上处理，不会传出设备。
  • 相册（添加）：把你拍的照片存进系统相册。
  • 相册（读取）：在 Circa 自己的图库里显示你用 Circa 拍的照片。Circa 会记一份这些照片的编号，方便再找到它们；不会读取、复制或发送其它照片。
  • 定位（仅在使用 App 期间，可不开）：把拍摄地点写进这张照片里，和系统相机的做法一样。Circa 不保存位置记录，也不把位置用于任何其它用途。

每项权限都由 iOS 弹窗询问，你随时可以在「设置 > Circa」里关掉。关掉定位后 Circa 照常能拍，照片只是不带地点。

存了什么、存在哪

所有东西都只在你的 iPhone 上：系统相册里的照片、那份照片编号，以及你在 Circa 里的设置（比如上次选的年代和画幅）。Circa 不会把这些发给开发者或任何人。如果你开了 iCloud 照片或设备备份，照片可能会进入你自己的 iCloud 或备份，这部分由 Apple 按 Apple 的隐私政策处理。

分享照片

Circa 本身没有分享功能。从系统相册分享照片时，地点会跟着照片一起发出去，除非你在分享面板的「选项」里关掉。

删除数据

删除 Circa 会一并删掉它的照片编号和设置。你拍的照片仍在系统相册里，要在相册里删除。

追踪与第三方

Circa 不追踪你，不含会收集数据的第三方代码，也不向任何人出售或分享数据。

儿童

Circa 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，新版本会发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。

联系方式

隐私相关的问题，可以通过 Circa 在 App Store 页面上的开发者联系方式发送；内测用户也可以在 TestFlight 里用「发送测试版反馈」。
